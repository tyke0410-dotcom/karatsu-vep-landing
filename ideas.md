## Chosen Approach: Ocean Bridge

**Design Movement**: Organic Editorial — a fusion of ocean-inspired organic forms with editorial magazine layout principles, evoking the connection between two island nations.

**Core Principles**:
1. **Fluid Asymmetry**: Sections flow like water — never rigid grids. Use diagonal cuts, offset columns, and organic curves.
2. **Depth Through Layering**: Overlapping cards, parallax images, and subtle shadows create a sense of dimension, like looking through ocean depths.
3. **Cultural Duality**: Visual elements echo both Japanese minimalism (ma, negative space) and Indonesian warmth (vibrant accents, batik-inspired patterns).
4. **Editorial Typography**: Large, confident display type paired with clean body text — magazine-grade hierarchy.

**Color Philosophy**:
- Primary: Deep ocean teal (`oklch(0.35 0.09 200)`) — represents the sea connecting Japan and Indonesia
- Accent: Warm coral (`oklch(0.68 0.18 35)`) — energy, youth, Indonesian warmth
- Neutral base: Warm off-white (`oklch(0.98 0.005 80)`) — Japanese paper aesthetic
- Dark text: Deep slate (`oklch(0.25 0.02 250)`) — readability and grounding
- The palette evokes a sunset over the ocean — the moment of encounter between two worlds.

**Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric editorial — sections alternate between full-bleed and contained content, with diagonal section dividers and offset image placements. No rigid centered grids.

**Signature Elements**:
1. **Wave Dividers**: SVG wave shapes between sections, symbolizing the ocean bridge
2. **Dual-Flag Motif**: Subtle visual cues referencing Japan and Indonesia (cherry blossom + batik patterns as decorative accents)
3. **Timeline Ribbon**: The Month 1 / Month 2 curriculum displayed as a flowing horizontal timeline

**Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth, fluid transitions that mirror the ocean theme. Hover states ripple gently. Language toggle feels like a tide shift — content fades and re-emerges.

**Animation**:
- Section reveals: gentle fade-up with 200ms stagger
- Wave dividers: subtle horizontal drift on scroll
- Language toggle: 300ms crossfade between JA/EN content
- CTA buttons: scale(0.97) on active, ripple effect on click
- Curriculum timeline: progressive reveal as user scrolls

**Typography System**:
- Display: "Outfit" (700/800) — modern, confident, geometric
- Body: "Noto Sans JP" (400/500) — clean, readable, supports both JA and EN
- Accent: "Outfit" (600) for labels and eyebrows
- Hierarchy: 64px > 40px > 28px > 20px > 16px

**Brand Essence**: A virtual bridge between two island nations, where young people discover a new world from home. For curious high school students. Different because it's real connection, not tourism.

**Brand Voice**:
- Headlines: Direct, warm, inviting — never corporate
- CTAs: Action-oriented but gentle
- Example lines:
  - "Zoomをつなげば、そこはもう海外。" / "Connect on Zoom, and you're already overseas."
  - "世界への一歩を、ここから踏み出そう。" / "Take Your First Step Into the World, Starting Here."

**Wordmark & Logo**: "Encounter a New World" in Outfit 800 with a subtle wave underline — the "W" in "World" extends into a gentle wave shape.

**Signature Brand Color**: Deep ocean teal — `oklch(0.35 0.09 200)`
